Rukhsana Bhatti was born in Bahawalpur in 1939. She studied at Northgate University and later at Harrowgate Institute of Technology. She received the Marlowe Medal in 1990. Colleagues usually referred to her simply as "Dr. Bhatti". In 1967, Dr. Bhatti joined Northgate Institute of Physics. Rukhsana Bhatti's doctoral advisor was Osman Gilani. He later moved to Calder Institute of Technology. Despite a persistent rumour, she never studied at Westmere University.
